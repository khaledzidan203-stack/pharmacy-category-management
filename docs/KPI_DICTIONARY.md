# KPI Dictionary — Pharmacy E-commerce Category Management

This document defines the commercial, inventory, supplier, and category-management KPIs used across the portfolio project.

> All examples are designed for synthetic / portfolio data. Definitions should be validated against each organization's business rules before production use.

## Commercial KPIs

| KPI | Definition | Formula / Logic | Business Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Net Sales | Sales value after applicable reductions in the analytical dataset | `SUM(NetSales)` | Core commercial performance |
| Units Sold | Total quantity sold | `SUM(UnitsSold)` | Demand and velocity analysis |
| Sales Contribution % | Share of relevant category / portfolio sales | `Entity Sales / Total Relevant Sales * 100` | Category and SKU importance |
| Gross Margin Value | Sales less cost of goods sold | `Net Sales - COGS` | Profit contribution |
| Gross Margin % | Margin as a percentage of net sales | `Gross Margin / Net Sales * 100` | Profitability quality |
| Average Selling Price | Average realized price per unit | `Net Sales / Units Sold` | Pricing and mix analysis |
| Sales Growth % | Change vs comparison period | `(Current - Previous) / Previous * 100` | Trend monitoring |
| Target Achievement % | Actual performance vs target | `Actual / Target * 100` | Performance management |
| Target Variance | Absolute difference from target | `Actual - Target` | Gap analysis |

## Assortment KPIs

| KPI | Definition | Formula / Logic | Business Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| ROS | Rate of Sales across selling stores | `Units Sold / Stores Selling` | SKU velocity |
| Distribution % | Share of eligible stores selling the SKU | `Stores Selling / Eligible Stores * 100` | Assortment reach |
| ABC Class | Contribution-based segmentation | Cumulative contribution thresholds | Prioritization |
| Add Candidate | Potential assortment addition | High demand / availability / category gap logic | Range expansion |
| Keep Candidate | Healthy assortment item | Balanced sales, margin, stock and availability | Maintain range |
| Remove Candidate | Rationalization candidate | Weak velocity + excess stock + low contribution | Range optimization |

## Inventory KPIs

| KPI | Definition | Formula / Logic | Business Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stock on Hand | Current physical/system stock quantity | `SUM(OnHandQty)` | Availability |
| Stock Value | Inventory investment at cost | `SUM(OnHandQty * UnitCost)` | Working capital |
| Days of Coverage | Estimated days inventory can support demand | `OnHandQty / Avg Daily Sales` | Replenishment / excess stock |
| OOS Flag | SKU is unavailable while expected demand exists | Business-rule flag | Lost-sales risk |
| Overstock Flag | Coverage above configured threshold | `DOC > threshold` | Working-capital risk |
| Dead Stock | Stock with no qualifying recent sales | Business-rule window | Disposal / markdown / transfer review |
| Near Expiry | Stock inside configured expiry horizon | `ExpiryDate <= SnapshotDate + horizon` | Expiry-loss prevention |
| Inventory Turnover | Cost movement relative to average inventory | `COGS / Avg Inventory Cost` | Inventory efficiency |
| GMROI | Gross margin generated per unit of inventory investment | `Gross Margin / Avg Inventory Cost` | Commercial inventory productivity |

## Supplier KPIs

| KPI | Definition | Formula / Logic | Business Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Supplier Sales Contribution % | Sales generated by supplier's SKUs | `Supplier Sales / Total Sales * 100` | Vendor importance |
| Supplier Margin Contribution % | Margin generated by supplier's SKUs | `Supplier Margin / Total Margin * 100` | Profit contribution |
| PO Value | Purchase order value | `SUM(OrderedQty * UnitCost)` | Spend analysis |
| Average Lead Time | Mean time from PO to receipt | `AVG(ReceiptDate - OrderDate)` | Supply reliability |
| Fulfillment % | Quantity received vs ordered | `ReceivedQty / OrderedQty * 100` | Service level |
| Supplier Dependency % | Share of category supplied by one vendor | Vendor share of sales / purchases | Concentration risk |

## Pricing & Promotion KPIs

| KPI | Definition | Formula / Logic | Business Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unit Margin | Profit per unit | `Selling Price - Unit Cost` | SKU profitability |
| Margin Leakage Flag | Margin below configured threshold | Business-rule flag | Pricing review |
| Discount % | Reduction from reference price | `(Reference Price - Selling Price) / Reference Price * 100` | Promotion depth |
| Sales Uplift % | Change during promotion vs baseline | `(Promo Sales - Baseline Sales) / Baseline Sales * 100` | Promotion performance |
| Unit Uplift % | Unit-volume change vs baseline | Same logic on units | Demand impact |
| Margin Impact | Change in gross margin during promotion | `Promo Margin - Baseline Margin` | Profit protection |

## Data Quality Rules

- Zero denominators return `NULL` / `N/A`, not misleading percentages.
- KPI logic must use consistent date, branch, category and SKU filters.
- Negative sales / returns should be retained and classified according to business rules rather than silently removed.
- Missing master-data mappings must be surfaced as data-quality exceptions.
- Near-expiry and dead-stock horizons must be configurable.
- Promotion uplift is descriptive unless an appropriate causal comparison design exists.
